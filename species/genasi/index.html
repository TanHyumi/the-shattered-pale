    Genasi¶

    Species Traits¶

    • Languages: Primordial
    • Lifespan: Adulthood - 30+, Life Expectancy - 150
    • Common Names: Varash, Raithu, Minali, Suryak

    tags: race¶

    Description¶

    Genasi are the first descendants of the Primordial titans, the godlike beings who shaped the world long before written history. As one of the three Elder Races, they once stood alongside the Aasimar and Dragonborn in unity. Their three subraces, Flora, Fauna, and Terrana, each carry the spark of a different titan, giving them a natural tie to the lands and living forces their ancestors watched over.

    Since the war, the Genasi have suffered more than most. Their cities fell, their numbers dwindled, and many were forced into a nomadic life. Today, most Genasi travel in small tribes, moving with the seasons and relying on tight‑knit communities for survival. Only a few settlements remain fully under their control, and even those are scattered across distant regions. Though they once held vast territories, Genasi are now found all over the world, often far from the lands their subraces once called home.

    Physical Traits¶

    Genasi generally resemble [Humans](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20(Wiki%20Home) in shape and appearance but carry clear signs of their Primordial heritage. Flora Genasi may show hints of growth or bloom, Fauna Genasi carry animal‑like vitality or features, and Terrana Genasi show the strength and stillness of stone and soil. All subraces have a wide range of skin and eye colors, from blue to yellow to grey, usually marking their titan heritage.

    Subraces¶

    • Flora Genasi - Shaped by the Primordial of growth and bloom. They are calm, patient, and steady, with small signs of plant life appearing in their hair, skin, or scent.

    • Fauna Genasi - Descended from the Primordial of living creatures. They are instinctive, energetic, and adaptable, often showing faint animal traits like sharper senses or subtle markings.

    • Terrana Genasi - Born from the Primordial of all non‑living matter. They are steady, resilient, and slow to bend. Their features may hint at stone, metal, sand, or even water‑smooth skin and tide‑cool blood, marking them as children of the earth in all its forms.
