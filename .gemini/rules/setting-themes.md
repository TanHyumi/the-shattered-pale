# Worldbuilding & Lore Rules

## Core Settings & Geography
- **World:** Raasha
- **Setting Name:** The Shattered Pale
- **Current Active Campaign Region:** The Emerald Reach
- **Cosmology:** Four distinct pantheons, each with multiple deities; species originate either via direct divine creation or natural/magical emergence.

## Key Factions & Aesthetics
- **Tone:** Frontier exploration, high-stakes political and faction conflicts, gritty rules and low-magic.

## Writing Directives
- Keep boxed read-aloud text under 3 sensory sentences.
- Focus on actionable GM tools over dense history essays.
