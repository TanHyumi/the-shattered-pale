# Commercial Strategy & Publishing Directives

## Product Architecture (Hub & Spoke Model)
- **Spokes (Entry Points):** Standalone, plug-and-play regional modules (e.g., *The Emerald Reach*) highlighting specific mechanics (e.g., naval skill challenges).
- **Hub (Flagship):** Comprehensive *The Shattered Pale* setting sourcebook released after building an established audience.

## Licensing & Compliance
- Rely strictly on SRD 5.2 (CC-BY-4.0).
- Use descriptive compatibility terms: *"Compatible with the 5th Edition Ruleset"*. Do not use official logos.
