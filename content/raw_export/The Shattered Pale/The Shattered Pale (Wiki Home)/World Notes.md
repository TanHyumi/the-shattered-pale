Age of Cosmos
-------------

1.  Group of [Aestios](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Pantheons/Aestios.md) (celestials) abscond from their community.
    
    1.  The celestials come from a nomadic group of star-powered gods, the [Aestios](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Pantheons/Aestios.md). They created constellations as ways of traversing the universe.
        
    2.  Using certain types of magic was forbidden, like using it to create new life or end conflicts -- neutrality was law.
        
2.  Celestials come to Raasha, which is inhabited by ancients -- The [Anazuatl](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Pantheons/Anazuatl/Anazuatl.md) (dragons) and [Dwasti](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Pantheons/Dwasti.md) (primordials).
    
    1.  Dragons were the masters of arcane magic, primordials of primal magic, and celestials of divine magic.
        
3.  A peace pact was signed by the three groups. In exchange for a residency on the planet, the celestials would teach the dragons and primordials how to create new life.
    
4.  The newly formed trinity of godlike beings create their first kind -- the Deva.
