Description
-----------

Tieflings are mortals imbued with the covert, cosmic essence of the [Ziratu](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Pantheons/Ziratu.md)—the secretive celestial pursuers who infiltrated Raasha before [The War](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Historical%20Events/The%20War%20for%20Raasha.md). While the world often misinterprets their striking physical features as demonic, their true lineage traces back to cosmic hunters who walked hidden among early humanoids. Though the [Ziratu](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Pantheons/Ziratu.md) have long since been sealed behind the Veil, Tieflings inherit an innate instinct for uncovering secrets, reading subtle intentions, and navigating shadow.

Most hail from the sun-cured coastal trade hubs and grand oasis city-states of southern [Soland](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Soland.md), across the sea from the [Aasimar](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Races/Aasimar.md) realms. While often viewed with suspicion or prejudice due to their ancestors' role in [The Great War](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Historical%20Events/The%20War%20for%20Raasha.md), Tieflings tend to be pragmatists and charming. Resilient and self-reliant, they value cunning, loyalty to chosen kin, and surviving in a world that fears their origins.

Physical Traits
---------------

Tieflings bear striking markers of their ancestors' hidden nature, characterized by sharp features, solid-colored obsidian or opalescent eyes, and prominent horns that sweep from the brow or temple. Skin tones range from rich terracotta and sun-bleached copper to deep indigo or ash. Some Tieflings are born with prehensile tails, though just as many possess none. As a Tiefling ages, their horns grow in size and complexity—developing intricate ridges, spirals, or heavy crests over decades that serve as a physical record of their longevity and maturity.

Subraces
--------

Tieflings have no subraces.
