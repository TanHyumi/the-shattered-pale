Description
-----------

Orcish folktales describe a time when their ancestors weren't people at all, but fearsome monsters known as Oni who roamed throughout [Tsugash](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Tsugash.md) in violent rages. These traditional stories passed down for generations say that one by one, Oni awakened to clarity and consciousness. From this mysterious surge of enlightenment came the first Orcs, who chose to pursue a path of discipline, tempering their ancestral power with purpose and honor.

A history of defending their homeland from would-be conquerors has shaped a culture among Tsugash Orcs where mastery of the blade became essential for survival. Today, Orc clans have slowly descended into civil war, out of differing political opinions, old grudges, or pride. Yet Orc folklore warns that endless bloodshed risks awakening the old nature of the Oni they once were, a superstition taken seriously by many clans. Because of this, a growing number of Orcs strive for peace, believing that honor is found in temperance rather than victory.

Physical Traits
---------------

Orcs are tall and broad‑framed, with tusks, pointed ears, and pronounced brow and jaw lines. Their skin ranges from grey to muted greens and blues, and their eyes are often bright warm colors like orange or yellow. Although many on Raasha think of Orcs as brutish because of their appearance, their introspective and refined natures show a people who value restraint, reflection, and the mastery of themselves.

Subraces
--------

Orcs have no subraces.
