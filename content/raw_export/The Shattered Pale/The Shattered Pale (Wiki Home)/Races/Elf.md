Description
-----------

Elves are magical beings born of materialized spirits. When creatures on Raasha die, traces of their memories and experiences linger in the spiritual world. Ages ago, a powerful sorceress learned to harness this essence and reform it into new life. It remains a sacred ritual still practiced today. When an Elf is born naturally instead, they inherit fragments of memories directly from their family line.

Connected to the spirit world, Elves possess an innate magic that lets them sense and shape its currents. Their collective memories give them a deep appreciation for knowledge and a reverence for tradition. Like Dwarves, most Elves rarely stray far from their homelands. They often speak in a thoughtful, measured tone, and their spiritual awareness lends them a serene, calming presence.

Physical Traits
---------------

Elves are slightly smaller than average humanoids due to their slender frames. While they resemble Humans in many ways, they have pointed ears, sharp features, and an ageless appearance. Remarkably agile and light on their feet, their movements carry an effortless grace.

Subraces
--------

*   **Dreamwalkers** \- When they dream, Dreamwalker Elves sometimes live through the memories of spirits. They perceive these dreams in real time, with a single rest feeling like days or weeks spent living another's long-lost life. This constant influx of historical perspectives leaves them deeply introspective, possessing an uncanny wisdom. Sometimes called Night Elves or _Drow_, they have skin in grayish hues of blue or purple.
    

*   **Fateweavers** \- By studying the flow of the spirit world, Fateweaver Elves read the collective mass of Raasha's memories to divine what is to come. Viewing history as a repeating tapestry, they sense the spiritual momentum of past choices to anticipate danger before it strikes. Commonly called High Elves, they are typically taller than other Elves and possess a calculating foresight.
    
*   **Spiritspeakers** \- Attuned to forgotten voices, Spiritspeaker Elves act as direct bridges between the living world and wandering spirits. Through painted runes or carved totems, they can allow the dead to speak through them or perform rituals to commune with nature spirits. Commonly known as Wood Elves due to their northern forest kingdoms, they possess warm, earthy hair and skin tones.
