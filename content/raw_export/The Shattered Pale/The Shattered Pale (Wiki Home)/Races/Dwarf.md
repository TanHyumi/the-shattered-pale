Description
-----------

Dwarves are a hardy and intelligent people, renowned for their engineering skill and remarkable craftsmanship. Most Dwarven societies are self-sufficient and insulated, with the people departing from their cities mainly to trade.

Hailing from [Khor](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Khor.md), a land of harsh deserts and deep canyons, the Dwarves have carved out a bountiful life for themselves by inventing technology that helps them shape the land around them. Their reputation for being cold or selfish comes from their focus on their own kin and allies, valuing others based on usefulness and proven commitment. However, their destructive inventions have also given them a long‑standing stereotype as warmongers, though most see their work simply as tools for survival.

Physical Traits
---------------

Dwarves are short and stocky, and live longer lives than most. Their ever-growing hair is usually neatly maintained and often decorated with small baubles that show status or wealth. They are sturdy in build, practical in action, and carry an unshakable confidence.

Subraces
--------

*   **Mountain Dwarves** - Born in the high peaks and deep canyon fortresses of Khor, they are steady, guarded, and slow to trust, but unmatched in stonework and engineering. Mountain Dwarves usually have fair skin and light eyes, shaped by generations spent underground and away from the sun.
    
*   **Dune Dwarves** - Shaped by the harsh dunes and shifting heat of Khor’s lowlands, they are resourceful, quick‑thinking, and skilled at crafting tools that survive extreme conditions. Dune Dwarves often have dark skin and hair, but their eyes are strikingly colorful.
