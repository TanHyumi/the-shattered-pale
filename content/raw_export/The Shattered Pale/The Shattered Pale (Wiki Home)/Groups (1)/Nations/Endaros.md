Overview
========

Overview
--------

Endaros is a nation that controls a large portion of [Ardona](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Ardona.md), primarily in the south. It was originally settled by Humans and remains predominantly [Human](undefined.md) in population. However, other species also reside there—most notably, the [Genasi](undefined.md).

While Endaros maintains peaceful relations with its southern neighbors in [Soland](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Soland.md), trade and travel across Athera's Strait remains rare. Endaran Humans tend to harbor lingering resentment toward the [Aasimar](undefined.md) due to the banishment that happened in generations past. Tensions are notably higher with the [Human](undefined.md) nation of [Bellatora](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Groups%20\(1\)/Nations/Bellatora.md), whose northern provinces seceded from Endaros following a civil war. The border between them remains unstable and ever-shifting.

History
-------

Endaros is the first nation settled by [Humans](undefined.md) and one of two nations that controls [Ardona](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Ardona.md). After a cohort of [Aasimar](undefined.md) were severed from their divine ancestry and stripped of their access to magic, they were banished from [Soland](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Soland.md) and sent north towards [Ardona](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Ardona.md). Their original settlement in Southern Ardona grew into what is now Endaros.
