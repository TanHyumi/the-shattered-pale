Description
-----------

Far to the east of [Soland](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Soland.md) and [Sa Zortao](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Sa%20Zortao.md), and standing as a sister‑continent to [Khor](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Khor.md), lies Tsugash, a land known for its beauty and bloodshed. It features emerald forests, mist-laden coasts, and old and snowcapped peaks. Despite the natural splendor, the people of Tsugash live in a state of tension. Clans, city-states, and wandering war bands clash over old grudges and diverging beliefs. The continent’s story is one of breathtaking abundance paired with unending struggle.

Climate
-------

Though some regions lean temperate, all four seasons arrive in full force across Tsugash. Winters sweep down from the northern ranges, coating the land in snow and turning mountain passes into treacherous corridors. Spring bursts forth with wildflowers and rushing rivers that carve through fertile valleys. Summers are warm and bright, drawing laborers into the open fields. Autumn sees that the forests turn red and gold before the first snows return.

Inhabitants
-----------

Tsugash is home to many peoples, but [Orcs](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Races/Orc.md) and [Goliaths](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Races/Goliath.md) form its cultural backbone. Their societies are as varied as the terrain they inhabit. Ancient Orcish clans trace their bloodlines back centuries, guarding traditions of honor, ancestor worship, and martial discipline. [Goliaths](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Races/Goliath.md) are a relatively new inhabitant, immigrating to Tsugash after the war, and causing some discontent in the process. Over time, Orcish clans have lost power as sovereign democracies form, but some refuse to lose the power they obtained generations ago.
