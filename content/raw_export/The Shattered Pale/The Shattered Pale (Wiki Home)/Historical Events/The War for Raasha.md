Overview
--------

The War for Raasha was a planet-spanning conflict that shattered the ancient age of divine harmony. Triggered by a covert betrayal among the gods, the decades-long war devastated mortal civilizations, scarred the realm's natural landscapes, and permanently severed the deities from walking directly alongside their creations.

Background
----------

Following the pact between the pantheons, three elder races were brought into existence: the [Aasimar](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Races/Aasimar.md) of the [Aestios](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Pantheons/Aestios.md), the [Dragonborn](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Races/Dragonborn.md) of the [Anazuatl](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Pantheons/Anazuatl/Anazuatl.md), and the [Genasi](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Races/Genasi.md) of the [Dwasti](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Pantheons/Dwasti.md). For an era, these lineages flourished under the direct guidance of their creators, establishing grand civilizations before giving rise to subsequent mortal races.

Unbeknownst to the realm, however, an enigmatic faction of celestial pursuers—the former clan of the [Aestios](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Pantheons/Aestios.md)—had tracked the renegades to Raasha. Watching from the shadows, these pursuers secretly infiltrated both mortal societies and the native pantheons, subtly sowing discord, jealousy, and suspicion.

History
-------

The secret orchestration culminated in a catastrophic betrayal, turning factions within the native pantheons against the [Aestios](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Pantheons/Aestios.md) and plunging the entire planet into war. Spanning decades, the conflict saw the rise and fall of countless mortal alliances, the destruction of ancient cities, and the divine empowerment or arcane ascension of extraordinary individuals who claimed godhood amidst the chaos.

As the catastrophic power of the gods irreversibly scarred the land and fractured the bonds between races, a desperate coalition of mortals took drastic action. Prioritizing the planet's survival over divine loyalty, this group enacted a grand ritual that sealed all pantheons away from Raasha behind an impenetrable cosmic veil.

In the post-war era, the physical presence of the gods on Raasha came to an end. While their power still bleeds through the veil—allowing clerics, warlocks, and scholars to tap into divine magic and ancient secrets—the deities can no longer walk among mortals, leaving a scarred world to rebuild in their shadow.
