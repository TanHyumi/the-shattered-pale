Session #1 (2026-08-14) — All Aboard
------------------------------------

### Background

*   For the last few months, Dulyn Sopuzzo of Sopuzzo Mercantile has been preparing for a voyage to the Emerald Reach. He's hired Captain Romega and a crew to help him find the fabled Sunken Tower — local folktales tell of a powerful Dragonborn sorcerer who built the tower so he could commune with the gods, and many say it's full of powerful, enchanted relics.
    
*   While Captain Romega was hiring a crew for The Wishbone, he put out specific postings for people who could protect the ship, her crew, and lead landing parties or scout ahead. Each member of The Party was individually hired as a member of that four-man crew.
    
*   The first goal of the voyage, in the eyes of Dulyn Sopuzzo, is to find the pieces of a celestial astrolabe, a tool that arcanists believe was used by the Dragonborn sorcerer. Dulyn believes it is the key to finding the tower.
    

### The Cache

*   After a few weeks at sea, The Wishbone's crew finally found one specific island Dulyn was looking for. He paid for a treasure map that detailed a hidden cache used by an old pirate lord and one piece of the astrolabe is believed to be there.
    
*   The Party was asked to accompany Dulyn to the island, and raid the cache. Once on shore, they found no signs of recent activity. After a brief time searching for the cache, said to be hidden between three fruit trees, U'll was able to locate it by climbing a large palm tree and surveying the small island. Rushing to the location, Dulyn started digging and promptly found a locked hatch. Haku broke the hatch, which opened to a short underground passage leading to the cache.
    
*   Once inside, the party found rotten food stores, old rum, and three medium-sized chests. Inside two were a pirate's haul: gems, jewelry, and other fine treasures. Inside the third was the astrolabe piece Dulyn was looking for — a brass ring, two feet in diameter, with carvings of the lunar cycle inside. The ring was wrapped in cloth and kept with a small scroll written in old Draconic. Dulyn mentioned to the party that Kaavik would be able to decipher its meaning with enough time.
    
*   As the party lifted the chests to leave, a trap was sprung! The support beams began to crumble, and toxic gas was filling the area. To survive the trap, the party needed to survive a skill challenge (DC: 4 successes, 3 failures). After 3 successes and 2 failures, everything fell on U'll's last roll. He cunningly produced a large gust of wind to briefly clear out the toxic gas, giving the party just enough time to escape with minimal treasure and the astrolabe piece.
    
*   While some precious treasure was lifted from the cache, Nix secretly gave himself the largest cut, leaving a bigger cut for The Party, and an even smaller cut for the rest of the crew. Nobody noticed, but Canto did see him alone, partitioning the treasure, causing him to metaphorically raise an eyebrow.
    

### Camping on the Beach

*   After the successful raid, Dulyn told the crew to anchor near the bay, and campout on the beach. They broke out some extra rations and had a night of games, story, and drinking.
    
*   Noticing some of the sailors dueling each other with makeshift swords, Nix asked if he could duel the winner. Between trying to go easy on the man who had clearly not professionally handled a sword, and the distractions that U'll was making, the fight ended up being closer than Nix would have liked. In the last moment, Nix found an opportunity to score his last point, but a loss of temper caused him to strike the sailor with excessive force. Although he won the duel against an untrained opponent, he managed to make himself look like an unhinged member of the crew. Canto, Nix, and Haku all saw the damage he caused. Later in the evening, Canto brought up the fight, mentioning that truly mastering one's power is knowing when to use it, leaving Nix to contemplate his actions. Haku performed a small Lay On Hands to cure the sailor's broken ribs, leaving him feeling better, but gobsmacked at the miracle.
    
*   Gordot was telling stories to the crew, and Haku was able to better his relationship with Bensi, bonding over their shared Elven heritage.
    
*   In the middle of the night, U'll woke up to a heavy cloud of fog rolling in on the beach. Seeing that one of the crewman in the fog, he fired a fire arrow out to illuminate the area. He awakened The Party, Dulyn, and Captain Romega. Just as he went to alert the rest of the crew, a large gas cloud erupted where they were camping, keeping them all unconscious.
    
*   A fight with Lizardfolk wearing bone armor promptly broke out. The invaders were firing sleep darts and trying to subdue The Wishbone's crew. Haku nearly cleaved one Lizardfolk warrior in half, and Nix finished the job, but they were outnumbered. Dulyn and Captain Romega listened to the demands of their leader, a large Lizardfolk with white tattoos and a thick battle-axe on his back. Shortly after, the party was restrained, their heads covered with sacks, and they were moved onto a sea vessel.
    

### The Hunt

*   After being kidnapped and spending a few sightless days aboard the enemy's ship, The Party found themselves kneeling on a beach of charcoal colored sand. They heard some of their captors return to the ship after dropping them off, and some leave in other directions.
    
*   They eventually determined that they were along, and managed to break free of their restraints. U'll was the first to find the note and dagger left for them on the beach. The note read: "Your gear has been hidden in the jungle. For now, your mission is simple. Stay alive." They surveyed their surroundings, seeing the beach ascend into a thick jungle and terminate into cliffs on either side.
    
*   From the conversations they were able to pick up on the shore, and from the manner in which they were abducted, in conjunction with the note, the party inferred that the Lizardfolk meant to hunt them for sport.
    
*   After successfully finding their gear dangling above a quicksand pit, the party made for a section of rocky crags. U'll presumed there to be freshwater near the top. After nearly a day of traveling through the jungle to find their gear and freshwater, the party made camp in the crags, dreading the first strike...
    

### Summary

*   Dulyn Sopuzzo put together a crew to venture out into The Emerald Reach, searching for the mythic Sunken Tower.
    
*   The party's members are part of The Wishbone's crew, serving as soldiers and scouts that can protect the ship.
    
*   After finding a piece to an astrolabe, which Dulyn believes will help them find the tower, once fully assembled, the crew camped out on a remote island's beach.
    
*   They were attacked in the middle of the night by a group of extremist Lizardfolk who were wearing bone-armor.
    
*   Dulyn and Captain Romega surrendered on the beach after realizing they'd all die trying to fight back, and the entire crew was abducted.
    
*   After a few days of traveling on the Lizardfolk group's ship, the party found themselves stranded an island with charcoal-colored sand.
    
*   The Lizardfolk plan to hunt them, and their goal is to survive waves of attacks.
