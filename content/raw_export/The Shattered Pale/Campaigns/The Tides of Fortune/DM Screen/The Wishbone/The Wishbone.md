The Wishbone is [Dulyn Sopuzzo's](Campaigns/The%20Tides%20of%20Fortune/Campaign%20NPCs/Dulyn%20Sopuzzo.md) expedition vessel. It is two levels and features two main masts, a captain's cabin, two small crew quarters, a ballista, and 3 small hatches. The bottom level is exclusively for storage.

The crew is roughly 15 men.

*   [Gordot](Campaigns/The%20Tides%20of%20Fortune/Campaign%20NPCs/Gordot.md) (pilot)
