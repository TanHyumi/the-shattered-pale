Description
-----------

Ashrock Island is a small tropical island where [The Red Scales](Campaigns/The%20Tides%20of%20Fortune/Groups/The%20Red%20Scales.md) have a small outpost. It is also home to a temple of [Tsozost](Campaigns/The%20Tides%20of%20Fortune/Groups/Order%20of%20Tsozost.md).

  

  

,

DM Notes
========

Features:

1.  Black sand beach. This is where the Scales drop off their victims. It's about 30 feet wide and 80 feet deep, with a steep walk up towards the jungle and flanking cliffs.
    
2.  Freshwater spring. Here is a small spring about 10 feet in diameter. The spring is roughly 15 feet deep.
    
3.  Random Encounter
    
4.  Random Encounter
    
5.  Random Encounter
    
6.  Cliffside cave. There is a small cave hidden to unsuspecting eyes tucked into the cliff walls, and where some survivors make camp. Its entrance has a direct view of the large standalone cliffs where the Tsozost temple is.
    
7.  Temple of Tsozost. Here is a series of large standalone cliffs rising out of the ocean. When at the same level, a small entrance is visible.
    
8.  Random Encounter
    
9.  Red Scales outpost.
