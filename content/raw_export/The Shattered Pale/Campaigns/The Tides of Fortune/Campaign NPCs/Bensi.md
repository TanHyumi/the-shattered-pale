Overview
========

> _"Look out!"_

Description
-----------

Bensi is a young Elf boy aboard The Wishbone. He is quiet and reserved but always willing to help another crew mate. He's of average Elven height and size, with moderately dark skin and thick, curly, black hair.

Traits and Motivations
----------------------

*   Bensi is a hardworking and loyal sailor.
    
*   Those who have established a relationship with Bensi know he is an orphan and has moved around a lot.
    

Routine
-------

Bensi seems to shadow [Gordot](Campaigns/The%20Tides%20of%20Fortune/Campaign%20NPCs/Gordot.md) when not actively working. Otherwise he's always busy, known amongst the sailors to be one of the hardest workers in the crew.
