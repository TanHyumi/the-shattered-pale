Overview
========

> _"Please, speak."_

Description
-----------

Kaavik is a middle-aged Blue Dragonborn scholar. She is a seasoned but limited mage, and serves aboard The Wishbone as a chronicler and translator.

Traits and Motivations
----------------------

*   Kaavik is polite but cold.
    
*   She is an experienced scholar having worked for multiple [Sa Zortao](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Continents/Sa%20Zortao.md)\-based expeditions.
    

Routine
-------

*   Kaavik is usually the last to arrive at meal times, and keeps to herself nearly all the time.
