Overview
========

> _"No."_

Description
-----------

Captain Romega is a serious and intimidating navy veteran who works as [Dulyn Sopuzzo](Campaigns/The%20Tides%20of%20Fortune/Campaign%20NPCs/Dulyn%20Sopuzzo.md)'s captain on The Wishbone. He's of medium-short height, with a wiry build and many sun spots. He has short greying hair and a well-kept beard.

Traits and Motivations
----------------------

*   Romega expects a lot of his men, but never asks them to do anything he wouldn't do himself.
    
*   He's cautious and balances Dulyn's reckless tendencies aboard The Wishbone.
    

Routine
-------

Romega is always well-groomed but is never seen away from his post at the stern.
