> [!NOTE]
> Upload ginormous images to use them as maps in LegendKeeper.

Right-click a map to create a pin. (Try it on [The Realm of Tutoria](Misc/__Welcome%20to%20LegendKeeper__/The%20Realm%20of%20Tutoria/The%20Realm%20of%20Tutoria.md).)

![Image](https://assets.legendkeeper.com/6a5cd6a8-6e3e-4488-8841-9926f6c0aa11.png?w=1280 "Attachment")

You can also drag an element from the left sidebar onto the map to create a pin!
