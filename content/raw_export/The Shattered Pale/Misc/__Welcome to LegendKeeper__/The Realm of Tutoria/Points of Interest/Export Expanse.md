> [!NOTE]
> You can easily export and import your LegendKeeper projects.

LegendKeeper is a powerful, specialized tool, but we never want you to feel like your content is locked up.

You can export your written content to Markdown and HTML, and you can export all of your worldbuilding content as JSON or LK files.

![Image](https://assets.legendkeeper.com/5a2c6542-d1b9-4e71-adb8-fe7f56caf2a0.png?w=1280 "Attachment")

We also offer a variety of import options for easily bringing data into LK.
