Overview
========

> [!NOTE]
> Use tabs to explore different aspects of your Elements.

**Elements** can have multiple content tabs, giving you more powerful organization options.

For example, if you have a place called "The Moonlight Citadel", you can keep its lore and its map together as tabs inside one element.

Drag and drop tabs to re-order them.

You can link to a specific tab by @-mentioning an element and typing a colon (:) directly after the mention.
