### Various Tips  

*   You can drag and drop elements from the sidebar to re-arrange them, quickly add pins to your maps, create shortcuts, and add links to the right sidebar.  
    
*   You can drag and drop tabs to re-arrange them.  
    
*   Right-clicking on elements will bring up a context menu with options to edit, delete, customize permissions, and more.  
    
*   Hit 'Add Block' in the panel to the right to add useful metadata like tags, images, YouTube videos, etc.  
    
*   You can export your written content to HTML, Markdown, or an LK file using the 'Export' feature in your project settings in the top right.  
    
*   You can share your project with the world by hitting the share button in the top right and toggling "Share Project to Web" to get a sharing link.
