# The Arrival

---
tags: event
---
Overview
--------

The [Aestios](/setting/pantheons/aestios/) came to Raasha from distant galaxies, eventually forming an alliance with the resident titans, the [Dwasti](/setting/pantheons/dwasti/) and [Anazuatl](/setting/pantheons/anazuatl/). The three pantheons agreed to share stewardship over the planet and its resources.

Background
----------

Before the [Aestios](/setting/pantheons/aestios/) arrived, Raasha was in a state of chaos. The [Dwasti](/setting/pantheons/dwasti/) and [Anazuatl](/setting/pantheons/anazuatl/) had spent millennia on the planet, cycling between periods of peace and war. At the time, the planet's only inhabitants were non-sentient lifeforms and the gods themselves.

History
-------

Nobody knows exactly how Raasha was created, nor do they know who its first residents were — both of which remain subjects of great debate. Scholars fear the true answers have been lost or were never accurately recorded in the first place. Despite the wide variety of faiths, theories, and stories found across Raasha today, most assume that the planet's first residents were either the [Dwasti](/setting/pantheons/dwasti/) or the [Anazuatl](/setting/pantheons/anazuatl/).

It is well known that these two groups shared the planet for millennia. Possessing the might and immortality of gods, yet lacking the ability to create life, these supreme beings were trapped in a cycle: neither could fully destroy the other, and they did not know what to do with their gifts.

That changed when the [Aestios](/setting/pantheons/aestios/) arrived. Announcing themselves as celestial travelers who navigated by the constellations, they brought new possibilities to the realm. Though the natives of Raasha were initially skeptical, an alliance was forged after an unknown span of time. The [Aestios](/setting/pantheons/aestios/) offered a trade: they would teach the other gods how to create life, provided that the planet and its resources were shared equally among the three pantheons and their future offspring. The deal was struck, and for a time, the gods worked together in harmony.

???+ note "GM Secret"
    Very few know the hidden truth: the Aestios were renegades who fled their nomadic, star-traveling clan to find a world where they could seed new existence. Though creating life was strictly forbidden among their kind, they believed it was their sacred duty to pass on their celestial gifts and lineage. Unbeknownst to them, however, their escape did not go unnoticed—and they were followed.
