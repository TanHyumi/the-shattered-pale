# Ardona

Overview
========

>   

Description
-----------

Ardona is a small continent in the Northern Hemisphere, curving westward as it stretches north. Its southern edge meets the oceanic waters of Athera’s Strait, a natural divide from the continent of [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/). The terrain shifts from coastal cliffs and windswept lowlands to craggy highlands and deep glens, growing harsher the farther north one travels.

Though remote, Ardona is not stagnant. Trade routes hug the coast, and strategic passes in the narrowing north are fiercely contested.

Climate
-------

Ardona’s climate is a study in severity and contrast, defined by its tapering geography and relentless northern winds. The southern reaches, closest to Athera’s Strait, experience cool maritime conditions—frequent rain, biting gusts, and short summers that allow for hardy crops from rocky soil. Fog rolls in from the strait, cloaking the coastlines, while inland valleys remain damp year-round.

As one travels northward, the land rises and the climate grows increasingly harsh and unforgiving. Winters are long and brutal, with snowdrifts swallowing roads and frost overstaying its welcome. The highlands are home to violent storms, sudden temperature drops, and a near-constant wind that howls through passes and ravines. Even in summer, warmth is fleeting.

This climate has shaped a culture of resilience and austerity. Architecture favors thick stone walls and turf roofs; clothing is layered, woolen, and built for durability. The people of Ardona know the weather not as a condition, but as a constant presence and force to be respected. It is said that the wind remembers the betrayal that brought them here, and that the cold is the land’s way of keeping them humble.

Inhabitants
-----------

The Humans of Ardona are the descendants of [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/), cast down and stripped of their celestial birthright. Banished to the cold reaches of the north, they first settled in the southern lowlands. Over generations, they pushed northward, driven by ambition and resentment, taming the native fauna, and conquering scattered [Genasi](/setting/species/genasi/) clans who once roamed the highlands. These Primordial-born peoples were subdued and forcibly assimilated—now their customs survive only in fragments, buried beneath the dominant [Human](/setting/species/human/) culture.

But unity did not last. A brutal civil war split the exiled into two nations: the south, proud of its ancestral settlements and wary of change, and the north, forged in conquest and hardened by frontier life. Though they share blood, the two nations now stand divided by ideology, memory, and the lingering question of who truly carries the legacy of the fallen divine. The [Genasi](/setting/species/genasi/), though silenced, reside beneath the surface—neither fully erased nor entirely accepted.
