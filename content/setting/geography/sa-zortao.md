# Sa Zortao

Overview
========

>   

**Description**
---------------

To the southeast of [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/), across warm equatorial currents and shifting trade winds, lies Sa Zortao. It is dominated by dense jungles, where vines hang like curtains and the air hums with unseen life. Rivers carve through the forest's undergrowth like veins of silver, feeding sprawling wetlands. Beyond the rainforests, adventurous explorers can hike across volcanic cliffs or through grasslands nestled in mountain valleys.

**Climate**
-----------

Sa Zortao’s climate is a symphony of heat and moisture, shaped by its position in the southern tropics. The continent’s jungles flourish with humidity, where rain falls in thick sheets. Monsoon cycles bring floods, saturate the canopy, and summon blooms so vivid they seem otherworldly.

Highlands and volcanic plateaus offer cooler air, while savannas and dry forests stretch across the rain-shadowed interior, marked by seasonal fires and golden grasses. Coastal regions are warm and stormy, shaped by ocean currents and salt-laden winds. Across Sa Zortao, the climate is studied, woven into ritual calendars and agricultural cycles.

**Inhabitants**
---------------

The [Dragonborn](/setting/species/dragonborn/) of Sa Zortao descend from ancient bloodlines said to align with the sky-serpents of myth. They dwell in cities and villages carved into cliffs and jungle clearings, with stepped ziggurats, bustling plazas, and celestial observatories.

Festivals mark solstices and eclipses, when drums echo through the jungle and sacred fires burn for days.
