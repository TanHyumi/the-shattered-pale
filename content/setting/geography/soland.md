Description
-----------

South of the cold winds of [Ardona](/setting/geography/ardona/) lies Soland, a geographically varied continent of sunlit coasts, rolling grasslands, and dense woodlands. A vast inland sea cuts through its eastern half, bringing salty air and lucrative trade to the surrounding regions. Along its shores, maritime cities adorned with monuments to the gods thrive. Farther inland, beyond rocky peaks and winding valleys, stretch ancient and watchful woods.

Climate
-------

Shaped by its central sea and diverse topography, Soland offers many geographical settings. Around the inland sea and coastal regions, the air is warm and fragrant, with long, golden summers and rainy winters. Sea breezes carry warmth and moisture into the cities and smaller valleys, creating a sense of fertility and ease.

The central plateau features drier grasslands and deserts and is the home of nomadic groups, deserters, and those drawn to the frontier.

To the north, the terrain cools, giving way to temperate forests and misty hills. Here, the seasons shift more sharply. Autumns blaze with color, winters bring frost and silence, and spring arrives with a rush of green and birdsong. Rain is frequent but gentle, feeding moss-covered glades and verdant woodlands.

Inhabitants
-----------

Throughout Soland, especially along the coasts, are collections of city-states and nations. Although primarily made-up of [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/) and Elves, many [races](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\) and cultures are present. However, the largest sovereign state is a theocracy led by [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/) seers, where divine order rules and old traditions are held sacred.

Scattered deeper into the continent lie old Elven kingdoms, each with its own customs, courts, and tangled alliances. Some are serene and scholarly, others wild and warlike, but all are deeply tied to the land, its seasons, and its memory.
