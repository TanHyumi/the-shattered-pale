# Tiefling

### Species Traits

- **Languages:** Infernal
- **Lifespan:** Adulthood - 20+, Life Expectancy - 100
- **Common Names:** Zariq, Azzur, Safira, Kalia

Description
-----------

Tieflings are mortals imbued with the covert, cosmic essence of the [Ziratu](/setting/pantheons/ziratu/)—the secretive celestial pursuers who infiltrated Raasha before [The War](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\). While the world often misinterprets their striking physical features as demonic, their true lineage traces back to cosmic hunters who walked hidden among early humanoids. Though the [Ziratu](/setting/pantheons/ziratu/) have long since been sealed behind the Veil, Tieflings inherit an innate instinct for uncovering secrets, reading subtle intentions, and navigating shadow.

Most hail from the sun-cured coastal trade hubs and grand oasis city-states of southern [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/), across the sea from the [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/) realms. While often viewed with suspicion or prejudice due to their ancestors' role in [The Great War](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\), Tieflings tend to be pragmatists and charming. Resilient and self-reliant, they value cunning, loyalty to chosen kin, and surviving in a world that fears their origins.

Physical Traits
---------------

Tieflings bear striking markers of their ancestors' hidden nature, characterized by sharp features, solid-colored obsidian or opalescent eyes, and prominent horns that sweep from the brow or temple. Skin tones range from rich terracotta and sun-bleached copper to deep indigo or ash. Some Tieflings are born with prehensile tails, though just as many possess none. As a Tiefling ages, their horns grow in size and complexity—developing intricate ridges, spirals, or heavy crests over decades that serve as a physical record of their longevity and maturity.

Subraces
--------

Tieflings have no subraces.
