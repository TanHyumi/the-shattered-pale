# Aasimar

### Species Traits

- **Languages:** Celestial
- **Lifespan:** Adulthood - 40+, Life Expectancy - 200
- **Common Names:** Thaliorus, Elystria, Astrelius, Lurimaros

Description
-----------

Aasimar are angelic mortals infused with a divine connection to the stars and moons beyond Raasha, recognized by many as one of the three Elder Races. Though most cannot consciously wield their celestial gifts, all Aasimar experience prescient dreams that echo their cosmic heritage. Aasimar often look to the night's sky for guidance, divining with constellations and trying to connect with their creators.

Most hail from eastern [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/), their ancestral homeland, but they can be found throughout the civilized world. They are often perceived as condescending and aloof, even though they view themselves as an enlightened people. Relaxed, steady, and fearless in the face of uncertainty.

Physical Traits
---------------

Aasimar commonly bear features reminiscent of the cosmos: radiant eyes, metallic skin, or moonlit veins. In stature, they are generally comparable to other mortal peoples of Raasha. Each year an Aasimar lives, a faint star-shaped mark appears somewhere on their body. As these soft, dull lights accumulate with age, they form a personal tattoo-like constellation. Many spend their lives seeking a meaning hidden within their sign.

Subraces
--------

*   **Dawnborn** - Blessed by rising light, their cosmic features carry warm, gentle hues. They’re known for optimistic attitudes and a drive to explore and grow.
    
*   **Duskborn** - Shaped by twilight’s contrast, their dimmer features glow in cool tones. They tend to be reflective, patient, and drawn to a life of introspection and contemplation.
