Description
-----------

Saurians are often mistaken for [Dragonborn](/setting/species/dragonborn/) because of their scaled skin and shared Draconic tongue, but there is no known relation between the two peoples. Their true origin remains a mystery, with only scattered stories hinting at where they first emerged. Outsiders sometimes view Saurians as uncivilized or savage, yet most live peaceful lives in close‑knit communities that value nature, kinship, and simplicity over grandeur. Lizardfolk and Tortles primarily inhabit [The Emerald Reach](/modules/emerald-reach/gazetteer/overview/), a warm archipelago between [Sa Zortao](/setting/geography/sa-zortao/) and [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/), while Kobolds are found mostly throughout the deserts and mountains of [Khor](/setting/geography/khor/). While they originally hailed from [Sa Zortao](/setting/geography/sa-zortao/), wars with the [Dragonborn](/setting/species/dragonborn/) led to a large diaspora.

Saurian society is shaped by cooperation, practical skill, and a deep respect for the natural world. Their settlements are modest but well‑adapted to their environments, built from local materials and arranged around communal spaces where food, labor, and responsibility are shared. While each subrace (and each community) has its own customs and ways of life, all Saurians value self‑sufficiency and the bonds they share with their kin. Their communities may appear primitive to outsiders, but they are resilient, resourceful, and deeply rooted in the lands they inhabit.

Physical Traits
---------------

Saurians vary widely in appearance, but all share scaled skin, animalistic athleticism and senses, and features shaped by their environments. Together, they form a diverse but unified people whose forms reflect the places they call home.

Subraces
--------

*   **Lizardfolk** \- Patient hunters and swift swimmers with crocodile‑like bodies and eel‑shaped faces, known for their keen senses and strong connection to the wetlands and jungles.
    
*   **Tortle** \- Broad‑framed, slow‑moving people who resemble bipedal tortoises, valued for their calmness, sturdy shells, and steady presence within their island communities.
    
*   **Kobold** \- Small, quick, and clever desert and mountain dwellers who rely on tight‑knit packs, sharp instincts, and resourcefulness to survive in the harsh lands of [Khor](/setting/geography/khor/). They often live in subterranean communities, and they have quickly adapted to the cold dark.
