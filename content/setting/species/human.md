# Human

Description
-----------

Humans are the stubborn descendants of the [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/) who turned against their gods during the War and were stripped of their divine birthright. Banished from [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/) to the harsh northern continent of [Ardona](/setting/geography/ardona/) as punishment, they have quietly grown in isolation while maintaining tight-knit communities that have never forgotten their fallen past.

Though most remain in their northern homeland, adventurous or outcast Humans can be found across Raasha working as sailors, mercenaries, and laborers. Tempered by generations in [Ardona](/setting/geography/ardona/)’s unforgiving wilderness, Humans are notoriously persistent, driven, and quick to hold a grudge—yet deeply reliant on the strength of their own communities.

Physical Traits
---------------

Humans bear a striking resemblance to their [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/) ancestors, though they lack their cosmic longevity and star-touched features. Their skin, hair, and eye tones are more grounded and muted, and they tend to possess slightly broader, stockier builds than average [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/), while remaining comparable in stature to the other mortal peoples of Raasha.

Subraces
--------

Humans have no subraces.
