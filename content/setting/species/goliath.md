# Goliath

- **Languages:** Primordial, Giant

Description
-----------

Goliaths were the second race shaped by the old Primordial gods, created after the [Genasi](/setting/species/genasi/) but with a very different purpose. Where the [Genasi](/setting/species/genasi/) embodied the raw forces of the natural world, Goliaths were forged to endure those forces. Though they can wield elemental power, their true gift lies in surviving it, channeling it, and refusing to break beneath it. This distinction has long fueled a rivalry with the [Dragonborn](/setting/species/dragonborn/), a race they once warred against, whose own elemental abilities and imposing stature often invite comparison neither race appreciates.

Goliath culture is built on challenge, blunt honesty, and the pursuit of personal glory and exceptionalism. It is common—expected, even—for young Goliaths to leave their clans and seek a name for themselves. Long ago, their people were subjugated by the Giants, forced into labor and hardship until they eventually won their freedom. Because of this history, Goliaths still speak the Giant tongue, though many use it reluctantly. They are famously competitive, measuring themselves and others through feats of strength, endurance, or courage, and while they respect their ancient connection to the Primordials, they strongly dislike being compared to their Genasi kin.

Physical Traits
---------------

Goliaths are the largest of all humanoid races, towering over others with broad frames and powerful builds. Their skin ranges through shades of gray and brown, and their inky black eyes often carry speckles or thin veins of color that stand out against their features. Natural markings run across their skin in patterns reminiscent of tattoos, typically indicating the clan or family they come from.

Subraces
--------

*   **Volcanic** - Volcanic Goliaths are acclimated to eruption zones, enduring blistering heat, acidic steam, and toxic volcanic gases that would kill most others.
    
*   **Typhonic** - Typhonic Goliaths are shaped by storm fronts that feature freezing blizzards, sky‑splitting lightning, and thunderous shockwaves.
