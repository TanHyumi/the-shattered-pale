Overview
--------

Endaros is a nation that controls a large portion of [Ardona](/setting/geography/ardona/), primarily in the south. It was originally settled by Humans and remains predominantly [Human](/setting/species/human/) in population. However, other species also reside there—most notably, the [Genasi](/setting/species/genasi/).

While Endaros maintains peaceful relations with its southern neighbors in [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/), trade and travel across Athera's Strait remains rare. Endaran Humans tend to harbor lingering resentment toward the [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/) due to the banishment that happened in generations past. Tensions are notably higher with the [Human](/setting/species/human/) nation of [Bellatora](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20\(Wiki%20Home\)/Groups%20\(1\)/Nations/Bellatora.md), whose northern provinces seceded from Endaros following a civil war. The border between them remains unstable and ever-shifting.

History
-------

Endaros is the first nation settled by Humans and one of two nations that controls [Ardona](/setting/geography/ardona/). After a cohort of [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/) were severed from their divine ancestry and stripped of their access to magic, they were banished from [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/) and sent north towards [Ardona](/setting/geography/ardona/). Their original settlement in Southern Ardona grew into what is now Endaros.
