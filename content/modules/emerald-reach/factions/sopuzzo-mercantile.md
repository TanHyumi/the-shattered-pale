Overview

Description
-----------

A small trading company based out of eastern [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/), founded and currently run by [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/) businessman [Dulyn Sopuzzo](/modules/emerald-reach/npcs/dulyn-sopuzzo/).

Within the trading industry, Sopuzzo Mercantile primarily deals in selling rare and exotic pieces, and serving as a seafaring shipping partner.

Currently [Dulyn Sopuzzo](/modules/emerald-reach/npcs/dulyn-sopuzzo/) is funding and leading an expedition to [The Emerald Reach](/modules/emerald-reach/gazetteer/overview/). He and his crew are sailing out of [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/) aboard [The Wishbone](/modules/emerald-reach/vessels/the-wishbone/).

History
-------

Due to Dulyn's lack of restraint and eagerness to share, many have heard the story of how Sopuzzo Mercantile started. Dulyn spent all of his money on a small sailing boat, transporting spices along the inner Soland coast after finding it saved 1 day of land travel. Inch by inch and through relentless endurance, he expanded his company.

Structure
---------

Currently, [Dulyn Sopuzzo](/modules/emerald-reach/npcs/dulyn-sopuzzo/) is very involved in the business's operations. He employs a handful of captains, who maintain set shipping routes, while also financing (and often participating in) longer expeditions to procure rare merchandise.
