# Gordot

> **Occupation:** Pilot | **Summary:** An experienced Goliath pilot aboard The Wishbone.

---
tags: character
---
Overview
========

> _"So there I was, looking at the skeletal silhouette of a yellow bone wraith..."_

Description
-----------

Gordot is a middle-aged Goliath and maritime pilot. He's tall and muscular, due to his Goliath heritage, but the muscle definition is hidden underneath layers of soft fat. He is bald, with a forehead tattoo and a bushy beard. While most find him intimidating, many love him for his stories and bravery.

Traits and Motivations
----------------------

*   Backs down from no challenge.
    
*   Is protective of his friends and ship mates, especially [Bensi](/modules/emerald-reach/npcs/bensi/).
    

Routine
-------

Gordot is usually seen at the stern, piloting the ship.
