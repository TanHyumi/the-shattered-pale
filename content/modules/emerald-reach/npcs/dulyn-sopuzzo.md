# Dulyn Sopuzzo

> **Occupation:** Entrepreneur | **Summary:** An adventurous Aasimar businessman, financier and leader of The Wishbone.

---
tags: character

Overview
========

> _"You should have seen their faces when I landed my legendary Barrel Backhand."_

Description
-----------

Dulyn is a muscular, wide-framed man with a cocky and boisterous personality. As a successful businessman and self-proclaimed master of bare-knuckle boxing, Dulyn leads his crew aboard [The Wishbone](/modules/emerald-reach/vessels/the-wishbone/) into The Emerald Reach to find the fabled sunken tower.

Traits and Motivations
----------------------

*   Tireless, self-assured, and domineering.
    
*   Generally optimistic, but to be avoided if he does lose his temper.
    
*   Wants to be the most profitable businessman in Soland so he can fuel an expedition and treasure-hunting empire.
    

Routine
-------

Dulyn is known to sleep very little. He's up early, working out or planning his day, but retires to his chambers early in the evening.

He likes to converse and check-in with his subordinates, but rarely stays and chats for very long.
