# Gambling Games

1\. Dead Man’s Drop
-------------------

> **Flavor:** Named after drowning sailors, this game is all about knowing when to stop before you "drown."

*   **Equipment:** 3d6 per player
    
*   **The Bet:** Standard pot (e.g., 5 silver pieces each)
    
*   **Rules:**
    
    1.  Players roll all 3 dice in secret.
        
    2.  In turn order, each player can choose to **"Hold"** or **"Drop"** (reroll 1 die of their choice).
        
    3.  You can Drop up to twice per round.
        
    4.  **The Catch:** If any die ever rolls a **1**, it’s a _Dead Man_. That die is permanently locked at `1` and cannot be rerolled again.
        
    5.  Highest total sum wins the pot.
        

2\. Kraken’s Eye
----------------

> **Flavor:** A brutal, fast-paced game played on barrel heads during short breaks between shifts.

*   **Equipment:** 2d6 total (shared)
    
*   **The Bet:** 1 gold piece entry into a shared pot.
    
*   **Rules:**
    
    1.  The dealer rolls 2d6 into the center.
        
    2.  **Kraken’s Eye (Pair of 1s):** The house sweeps the pot immediately. Everyone loses.
        
    3.  **High Sea (Sum of 7 or 11):** The current roller takes the pot.
        
    4.  **The Trap (Pair of 6s):** Double the stakes! Everyone must match their initial bet to stay in, and the roller rolls again.
        
    5.  Any other roll passes the dice to the next player until someone wins or the Kraken takes it.
        

3\. Bilge Rats
--------------

> **Flavor:** Played with cards (or a Three-Dragon Ante deck). It’s quick, ruthless, and rewards a clean bluff.

*   **Equipment:** Standard card deck (Aces high)
    
*   **The Bet:** Anted coins (e.g., 2 copper pieces)
    
*   **Rules:**
    
    1.  Deal 2 cards face-down to each player and 1 card face-up in the middle (the "Bilge").
        
    2.  Each player secretly looks at their hand. The goal is to have the highest pair or highest single card, but **the Bilge card counts as a shared third card for everyone**.
        
    3.  Going around the table, players have two choices:
        
        *   **Bail:** Fold and lose your ante.
            
        *   **Match:** Pay another ante to reveal your hand.
            
    4.  Best 3-card combination (using your 2 cards + the Bilge) takes the pot.
        

4\. Skull & Cutlass
-------------------

> **Flavor:** A high-stakes push-your-luck game using a single 20-sided die. Popular among captains and officers.

*   **Equipment:** 1d20 per player
    
*   **The Bet:** Variable (Players increase the bet each push)
    
*   **Rules:**
    
    1.  Every player puts in a starting bet and rolls 1d20 face up.
        
    2.  On your turn, you can choose to **"Set Sail"** (stop and bank your score) or **"Push the Wind"** (roll another d20 and add it to your total).
        
    3.  You can roll as many times as you like, **but if your cumulative total exceeds 30, you "Capsize" and lose everything.**
        
    4.  Highest score under or equal to 30 wins. Tie breaks go to whoever reached the score with fewer rolls.
