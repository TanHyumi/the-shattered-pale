## 5: Develop Fantastic Locations

---
- **Cache**

The cache lies about a mile inland from the cove the crew stops at. Its entrance is a sand covered trapdoor set between 3 fruit trees. Going down puts you into a cramped hallway supported by beams and wooden walls which goes on for 30 feet before reaching a heavy wooden door with a simple lock.

- The door opens up to a cellar with 8 foot ceilings. Numerous rotted food stores are around, but some alcohol remains. There are also a handful of chests holding trinkets, gems, and jewelry, plus a few holding small arms.

- One smaller chest in the corner with an intricate lock holds part of an astrolabe. It is a heavy ring made of brass, about 2 feet in diameter, with carved moon patterns on the interior section. Along with it is a weathered parchment scroll written in old Draconic, detailing how this piece seems to be the key to locating the sunken tower.
