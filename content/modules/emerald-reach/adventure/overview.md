# Campaign Overview

## Setting Overview
- The campaign takes place in a sea between [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/) and [Sa Zortao](/setting/geography/sa-zortao/) known as [The Emerald Reach](/modules/emerald-reach/gazetteer/overview/). It features archipelagos and large islands.

The islands are full of primordial and draconic ruins.

- There are no large nations controlling the majority of the islands, aside from a few outposts that are closer to the divided continents.

- The permanent inhabitants of the islands include those who live in the established city-states, native tribes and clans, as well as pirates, criminals, and mercenaries.

- The sea is home to dangerous sea creatures and monsters.

## Campaign Overview
- Local folk throughout The Reach tell of a [Anazuatl](/setting/pantheons/anazuatl/) tower in [The Emerald Reach](/modules/emerald-reach/gazetteer/overview/). The stories tell of a wealthy [Dragonborn](/setting/species/dragonborn/) noble, and master mage, who lived there and spoke with the gods. Today, many believe that the tower sunk during the war but that it holds vast riches and powerful secrets.

- In recent decades, colonizers from [Soland](/setting/geography/soland/) have established some city-states in the area. Similarly, a Dwarven trading company from [Khor](/setting/geography/khor/) have recently arrived and are trying to gain a foothold in the area. The colonizers see The Reach as a good location for building trade cities and harvesting the local ore, Deep Steel, but the Dwarves have hidden motives -- some of the beaches in the Reach feature a dark crimson volcanic rock that, when ground down, melted, and turned into glass, can hold arcane charges.

- Recent activity has pushed some locals towards quiet revolution. They are awakening guardian spirits and leviathans which threaten merchants, colonizers, pirates, and natives alike.

## Factions/Groups
- [Order of Tsozost](/modules/emerald-reach/factions/order-of-tsozost/) - ancient group of empowered warriors that serve Tsozost.

- [Sopuzzo Mercantile](/modules/emerald-reach/factions/sopuzzo-mercantile/) - a small trading company owned by a wealthy [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/) magnate with an interest in relics.

- Radical Lizardfolk Clan, [The Red Scales](/modules/emerald-reach/factions/the-red-scales/) - they hunt humanoid prey as trophies and as a rite of adulthood, wearing armor made of bone.

- Coalition of City-States, [The Platinum League](/modules/emerald-reach/factions/the-platinum-league/) - a handful of city-states established in the region that have banded together in an effort to defend themselves from pirates, establish trade routes, etc.

- [Dwarven](The%20Shattered%20Pale%20(Wiki%20Home) Trading Company, [Western Khor Trading Company](/modules/emerald-reach/factions/western-khor-trading-company/) - Eastern-based company wanting to establish a presence amongst the islands for its resources. Known for being war-dogs.

- Pirate Lord, [Sinclair](/modules/emerald-reach/npcs/sinclair/) - the most dominant pirate lord in the region that is amassing a fleet of conquered pirate bands.

- Native Tribes - differing clans of [Saurian](/setting/species/saurian/) and [Genasi](/setting/species/genasi/) natives.

- [The Reefbound](Campaigns/The%20Tides%20of%20Fortune/Groups/The%20Reefbound.md) - local shamans and warriors practicing ancient ceremonies and warring against trespassers.

## Objectives/Threats
- Locate the sunken tower.

- Survive the roaming leviathan.

- Navigate faction tensions.
