# The Wishbone

The Wishbone is [Dulyn Sopuzzo's](Campaigns/The%20Tides%20of%20Fortune/Campaign%20NPCs/Dulyn%20Sopuzzo.md) expedition vessel. It is two levels and features two main masts, a captain's cabin, two small crew quarters, a ballista, and 3 small hatches. The bottom level is exclusively for storage.

The crew is roughly 15 men.

*   [Dulyn Sopuzzo](/modules/emerald-reach/npcs/dulyn-sopuzzo/) (financier and leader)
    
*   [Captain Romega](/modules/emerald-reach/npcs/captain-romega/) (captain)
    
*   [Gordot](/modules/emerald-reach/npcs/gordot/) (pilot)
    
*   [Kaavik](/modules/emerald-reach/npcs/kaavik/) (chronicler)
    
*   [Bensi](/modules/emerald-reach/npcs/bensi/) (barrelman)
    
*   Forward Party / Men-at-Arms (the party of 4)
    
*   General Deckhands + Craftsmen (6x)
