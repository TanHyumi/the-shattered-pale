# Haku

Haku is an [Orc](/setting/species/orc/)\-[Elf](/setting/species/elf/) trade liaison for an Orcish clan on [Tsugash](/setting/geography/tsugash/), searching for new trade opportunities for his clan to gain wealth and glory.

???+ note "GM Secret"
    Haku, the only child of Goraku, Chief of Clan Kaidokhan, and Saelindra, a high elf, faces a crisis as a valuable shipment, including a rare magical weapon, goes missing. This threatens his father’s trade empire and relationships with the dwarves and sovereign states. Tasked with uncovering the truth and restoring his father’s reputation, Haku must navigate the complex political landscape and prove his worth as heir.
