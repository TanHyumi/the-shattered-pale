# U'll

U'll is a [Genasi](/setting/species/genasi/) tracker and hunter, known for causing mischief, burying treasure, and crafting tools or weapons from foraged supplies.

???+ note "GM Secret"
    U’ll B. Shot is a Terrana Genasi forged from mud, stone, and tide, whose cold-blooded nature drives him to live off the land in complete poverty. Driven by a deep hatred for wealth and the environmental destruction it causes, he tracks down ancient relics and destroys or buries them to keep them out of greedy hands. When a wealthy syndicate launches a massive expedition for a game-changing artifact, U’ll infiltrates the heavily armed crew by posing as their reliable scout. He plans to guide them through the wilderness and let them do the heavy lifting of unearthing the prize. Once the artifact is recovered, he intends to turn his bow on his companions, execute them from the shadows, and hide the relic where no one will ever find it.
