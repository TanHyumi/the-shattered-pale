# Canto

Canto is a wandering [Human](/setting/species/human/) minstrel, with a smile and wit that can disarm almost anyone. His weathered exterior tells a story of a man who carries many burdens.

???+ note "GM Secret"
    Rising from a humble background, Canto used his charm, wit, and deceit to hide his origins and eventually become a personal advisor to a powerful aristocrat. However, his loyalty vanished when he witnessed the ruling class's cruelty toward the poor, culminating in the nobles ordering a friend's crops burned. Enraged by their contempt for lower classes, Canto attempted to assassinate him in his sleep, leaving him permanently mute before being captured. Sentenced to death, Canto endured ten years of brutal prison labor—kept sane only by playing salvaged instruments—while deepening his hatred for the throne. On the eve of his execution, he poisoned his guards, escaped into the shadows, and left a single mousetrap behind in his empty cell as a final message.
