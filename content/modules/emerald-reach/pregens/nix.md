# Nix

Nix is a [Aasimar](/setting/species/aasimar/) duelist from a well-regarded family. He carries a magical sword, intends to do well by those he meets, but sometimes loses control...

???+ note "GM Secret"
    Born into privilege as a son of House Vane, Nix secretly gave in to dark, demonic nightmares that offered him ultimate power instead of holy virtue. Embracing lethal fencing, dark magic, and a reckless addiction to high-stakes duels, his ambition culminated in killing Lord Julian Blackwood during a scandal-ridden sunrise duel. Forced to flee his family estate to evade Blackwood blood-feud assassins, Nix now poses as a wanderer while seeking the true source of his dark patron's strength. Torn between his divine legacy and his fiendish corruption, he embraces the deadly game, determined to achieve supreme power regardless of the odds or enemies in his way.
