Setting Overview
========

Gods, Dragons, and Primordials once walked among us. Their voices defined empires and carved mountains. But after a great war, they were sealed behind an arcane veil. Now, divine power trickles through only in fragments. Arcane knowledge and magical artifacts are feared, and the powers of the world fight for control. The scars of that war still burn across the land.

This is a world of fractured alliances, ancient relics, and mythic truths twisted by time.

Themes
======


#### Myth and Legend

*   Ancient entities and mighty gods may be sealed beyond this world, but their echos still linger. Behind every story is a truth to find... or fear.


#### Swashbuckling Exploration

*   Whether you're sailing uncharted waters or delving into ruins of forgotten civilizations, you will encounter enchanted artifacts, eldritch monsters, and rival pathfinders.
    

#### Dangerous Magic

*   For a time, powerful beings used magic to shape the world and its people, before passing it down to their students. Today, magic is rare and often unstable. Those who fight over its power will bear its consequences.
    

#### Political Intrigue

*   In the worlds of court and council or crime and corruption, only the shrewd, magnetic, or relentless can thrive -- it is here where your choices can shift alliances, shape nations, and end (or begin) wars.
    

Houserules
==========

#### Resting

*   **Short rests** require the party to take at least 8 hours of downtime, usually at the end of a day, during which they can perform only light activity. If this downtime is interrupted by more than a half hour of heavy activity (fighting, spellcasting, traveling, etc), the character gains no benefit from the rest.
    
*   **Long rests** require the party to take at least 48 hours of downtime in a safe area, during which they can perform normal downtime activities such as crafting, carousing, researching, gambling, shopping, light training, etc.
    

#### Death Saves

*   If a player needs to roll a **Death Save**, it is to be done in private -- neither the DM nor the other players should see or know the outcome.
    
*   Whenever you are making **Death Saves** and either **Stabilize** or **heal above 0 HP**, you receive **1 level of Exhaustion**. You cannot exceed 5 levels of Exhaustion in this way.
    
*   **Last Stand** \- Once per Long Rest, you can choose to use the Last Stand ability before rolling a Death Save. Doing so allows you to take an Action and move up to half your speed (in either order). During the Last Stand, you cannot regain any hit points or receive any temporary hit points. After the Last Stand ends, you automatically receive 2 failed Death Saves and must proceed to roll another Death Save before ending your turn.
    

#### Combat

*   **Tactical Initiative Shift** \- After initiative is rolled, the party may collectively decide whether half of the group (rounded down) will relinquish their rolled initiative scores. These characters immediately move to act directly before or after a chosen ally in the turn order. The characters choosing to move can only move _down_ in initiative. This decision must be made before any creature takes its first turn in combat.
    
*   **Desperate Casting** \- Once per short rest, a character with the **Spellcasting** feature may strain their magical reserves to regain a spell slot. As a bonus action, they recover one expended spell slot of their choice, up to 4th level, and immediately gain a number of levels of exhaustion equal to the level of the recovered spell. This ability cannot restore spell slots beyond the highest level the character can normally cast.
